Page 2: Key Target, FOXM1

HO-1197 directly inhibits FOXM1, the 'master regulator' of cancer proliferation, metastasis, and treatment resistance. This strategy disrupts the fundamental driving mechanism of the tumor.

[HO-1197] Inhibition → [FOXM1] → (PLK1, TTK and other downstream signalling)
↓
[Cell Cycle Arrest (G2/M Arrest)] + [Inhibition of Angiogenesis] + [Inhibition of Metastasis] + [Immune Activation]

Page 3: ROS-mediated DNA Damage

HO-1197 induces DNA damage by accumulating Reactive Oxygen Species (ROS) in cancer cells, which leads to apoptosis. This mechanism works independently of p53 mutation, making it effective even for tumors resistant to conventional therapies.

Page 4: Cell Cycle Arrest

HO-1197 downregulates mitosis-related genes to stop cancer cell proliferation. Specifically, it induces G2/M phase cell cycle arrest, disrupting the division process of cancer cells.

Page 5: Anti-angiogenic and Anti-metastatic Properties

HO-1197 inhibits the formation of new blood vessels, which are essential for tumor growth, and reduces the migration and invasion capabilities of cancer cells, thereby preventing metastasis.

Page 6: Immunomodulatory Effects

HO-1197 not only directly attacks cancer cells but also enhances anti-cancer effects by activating the body's immune system.

🛡️

M1 Macrophage Polarization

Reprograms tumor-promoting M2 macrophages into M1 macrophages that attack cancer cells.

💥

NK Cell Activation

Selectively enhances the anti-cancer activity of Natural Killer (NK) cells, especially those with low baseline function.

Page 8: Overwhelming Safety Profile

HO-1197 was proven safe and non-toxic in pre-clinical toxicity studies. Its ability to selectively kill cancer cells without toxicity to normal hepatocytes is a significant advantage over existing treatments.

Page 9: Synergy with Sorafenib
